# Social Media Growth Strategy & Content Masterplan for Pixel IT Thakurgaon

## 1. Executive Summary & Growth Objective

In the Bangladeshi computer retail market—especially in district hubs like Thakurgaon—social media is not just an awareness tool; it is a **direct sales engine (F-Commerce)**. Consumers in Rangpur Division rely heavily on Facebook Reels, Facebook Pages, local community groups, and YouTube Shorts to discover tech products, verify prices, and build trust before visiting a store or ordering online.

### Primary Objectives for Pixel IT:
1. **Drive Local Footfall:** Turn online viewers into walk-in buyers at Suruchi Super Market and Nishi Market outlets.
2. **Expand District Reach:** Capture buyers in surrounding upazilas (Pirganj, Baliadangi, Ranisankail, Haripur) for courier delivery (Cash on Delivery).
3. **Build Tech Authority:** Become the most trusted local technology brand in Thakurgaon by offering transparent pricing, authentic reviews, and technical advice.

## 3. How to Grow Pixel IT Using Social Media (Tactics & Execution)

### Tactic 1: Solve the "Price Inbox" Friction (Adopt Price Transparency)
* **The Problem:** Most Bangladeshi local pages write *"Price in Inbox"*, which frustrates consumers and slows down conversions.
* **The Growth Solution:** **Always display exact prices or price ranges in video captions and posts.** Price transparency builds instant credibility and attracts serious buyers who are ready to purchase immediately.

---

### Tactic 2: Master Short-Form Video Automation (Reels + Shorts)
* **Frequency:** 3 to 4 Facebook Reels per week.
* **Setup:** A simple smartphone with good lighting inside the Suruchi Super Market outlet and a cheap clip-on wireless microphone (e.g., K8 or Boya wireless mic, costing ~৳৮০০–১,২০০).
* **Cross-Posting:** Upload the exact same vertical video to:
  1. Facebook Reels
  2. YouTube Shorts (Optimized with search terms like "Thakurgaon laptop shop")
  3. TikTok (Growing tech user base in Rangpur division)
  4. Instagram Reels

---

### Tactic 3: Hyper-Local Targeted Facebook Ads Strategy
Do not waste ad spend targeting all of Bangladesh. Target Pixel IT's exact regional catchment area.

* **Audience Targeting Setup (Meta Ads Manager):**
  * **Location:** Pin drop on *Thakurgaon Sadar* + 30-mile radius (covers Pirganj, Baliadangi, Ranisankail, Haripur).
  * **Age:** 18 – 45.
  * **Interests:** Laptops, Computer Hardware, ASUS, Lenovo, Gaming, E-Commerce, Wi-Fi Routers.
* **Ad Objective:** **Messenger / WhatsApp Direct Messages** or **Traffic to `pixelit.com.bd`**.
* **Budget Recommendation:** $2 – $5 per day boosted on high-converting product videos (e.g., offer posts, laptop unboxings, or projector demos).

---

### Tactic 4: Automate Messenger & F-Commerce Sales Channel
When a potential buyer comments or messages after seeing a reel, speed matters.

1. **Automated Messenger Greeting:**
   * *"ধন্যবাদ পিক্সেল আইটিতে যোগাযোগ করার জন্য! আপনি কি ল্যাপটপ, রাউটার, নাকি সার্ভিসিং বিষয়ে জানতে চান?"*
   * Option buttons linking directly to:
     * 💻 [ল্যাপটপ ক্যাটালগ দেখুন (pixelit.com.bd)]
     * 🌐 [ওয়াইফাই রাউটার প্রাইজ]
     * 📍 [আমাদের শোরুমের লোকেশন]
     * 📞 [সরাসরি কথা বলুন]
2. **Instant Messenger-to-Website Handoff:**
   * Send the exact product link from `pixelit.com.bd` so users can inspect specs, order online, or visit the store.

---

### Tactic 5: Engage with Thakurgaon Local Facebook Groups
Assign the Digital Marketing Executive to actively participate in local community groups:
* *Thakurgaon Buy and Sell Groups*
* *Thakurgaon Helpline / Online Market Groups*
* *Thakurgaon Polytechnic & College Student Groups*

**Strategy:** Share helpful technical answers, unboxing videos, and official offer announcements directly in these groups (not spamming, but providing value).

## 5. Key Metrics for the Digital Marketing Executive to Track

To measure social media growth effectively, track these key metrics weekly:

1. **Local Reach & Impressions:** Number of unique users reached in Thakurgaon district.
2. **Video Views (Reels):** Total 3-second and 1-minute views on local unboxing reels.
3. **Inbound Messages:** Number of Facebook Messenger & WhatsApp product inquiries per week.
4. **Social-to-Web Traffic:** Volume of website visits on `pixelit.com.bd` originating from Facebook/Instagram links.
5. **In-Store Attribution:** Asking walk-in customers at Suruchi Super Market / Nishi Market: *"আমাদের কোন ভিডিও বা পোস্ট দেখে এসেছেন?"*
